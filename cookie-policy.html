Cookie Policy

We keep cookies to a minimum. This page explains what little we use and how you stay in control.

Last updated: 2 June 2026.

Template notice: this cookie policy is a general template. Please review it and adjust it to your actual setup before relying on it for a live website.

The short version

Passwords4Free sets no advertising and no cross-site tracking cookies. We do not profile you or sell your data. The only browser storage we use is optional and stays on your device.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file a website can store in your browser. Related technologies like localStorage work similarly — they let a site remember small pieces of information between page loads or visits. They can be useful (remembering a preference) or, in some cases, used to track people across sites. We only use them for the first kind of purpose.

Browser storage for preferences

Our tools may use your browser's localStorage to remember interface preferences — for example, your last-used generator settings — so the site behaves consistently when you return. This information stays in your browser, is not sent to us, and is not used to identify or track you. You can clear it at any time using your browser's settings.

Google Fonts

We load web fonts from Google Fonts so the site displays correctly. Depending on your browser and Google's configuration, Google may set cookies on its own domains or receive your IP address as part of serving those fonts. That activity happens under Google's control and is governed by Google's own policies, not ours.

How to control cookies

You are always in charge. Most browsers let you view, block, and delete cookies and clear site storage:

  • Open your browser's privacy or site-settings menu to block or delete cookies and clear stored data for a specific site.
  • Use private/incognito mode, which discards most storage when you close the window.
  • Clear "site data" or "browsing data" to remove anything we have saved in your browser.

Because our tools do not rely on tracking cookies, blocking them will not stop the generator or analyser from working — though clearing storage may reset your saved preferences.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
